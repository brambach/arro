Support

How can we help?

Saunafam is a weekly sauna streak for your family or your sauna crew. Everyone goes when they can, and you keep it together.

Need a hand? Emailsupport@saunafam.comBryce reads every one and usually answers within two days.

Questions

Does it work with Apple Watch?

No. Saunafam has its own timer on your iPhone, because the Watch isn’t made for sauna heat.

Do I need a home sauna?

No. Any sauna counts: the gym, a spa, a lake sauna, a friend’s.

Can friends join?

Yes. Family means whoever you sauna with, up to 12 people.

What does it cost?

It’s free for now.

Does it read my Health data?

No. If you turn it on, Saunafam saves your sessions to Apple Health as mindful minutes. It never reads from Health.

I used Arro. How do I turn off its old Health access?

iOS keeps the read permission Arro had, and the app can’t remove it for you. Open the Settings app, then Health, then Data Access & Devices, choose Saunafam and turn everything off.

Getting started

How do I start a family?

Open Saunafam, tap Start a family, sign in with Apple, name your family and pick your rhythm. Then send the invite from the app.

How do I join a family?

Ask whoever started it for an invite. Open Saunafam, tap I have an invite, sign in and enter the code. Codes last 14 days, so ask for a new one if yours has run out.

Does everyone need an iPhone?

For now, yes. Saunafam is iPhone only.

The streak

When does the family streak go up?

When everyone in the family keeps their week. By default that’s three sessions of ten minutes or more, Monday to Sunday. The person who started the family can change the rhythm.

What’s a freeze?

Everyone has one. If your week slips, it’s used for you, so the family streak keeps going.

I’m going away. What do I do?

Take a break in Settings, for the rest of the week or up to two weeks. Your family sees you’re on a break and when you’re back, and nobody asks why.

We sat together. Do we all log it?

No. One of you logs it and adds the others, up to four people, and it counts for everyone in the room. Anyone added can remove themselves.

I forgot to log one.

Log it for today or yesterday with Log one I already did. Nothing older, so nobody can backdate a week.

Your account

How do I delete my account?

Settings, Delete account. It removes your account, your sessions, photos, cheers and comments. See the privacy policy.

I got a new phone.

Install Saunafam and sign in with the same Apple ID. You’ll be back in your family.
