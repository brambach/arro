How it works

Close the door. We’ll keep time.

  1. Start the timer, or log it after.

    Close the door and start the timer, or log a session once you’re home. Ten minutes or more counts.

  2. Three a week each.

    Everyone aims for three sessions a week, whenever suits them. Your family can set its own rhythm, from two to five.

  3. One streak for all of you.

    When everyone keeps their week, the family streak grows by one. Nobody’s ranked, and nobody carries it alone.

The wall

Every week you keep adds a slat.

The streak is a wall of wooden slats, building up from the bottom. Every four weeks starts a new bench, so twelve weeks read as three.

Miss a week? Everyone has one freeze. Away? Take a break.

Kind by design

Made for the people you love, not a leaderboard.

  • No rankings

    Nobody’s first and nobody’s last. The streak belongs to all of you.

  • A missed week is never pinned on anyone

    If a week slips, Saunafam says the week slipped. It never says who.

  • Nudges are capped

    One a day and two a week at most, so a nudge stays a nudge.

  • No ads, no tracking

    No ads, no analytics, nothing sold. Your sessions stay in your family.

FAQ

Questions

Does it work with Apple Watch?

No. Saunafam has its own timer on your iPhone, because the Watch isn’t made for sauna heat.

Do I need a home sauna?

No. Any sauna counts: the gym, a spa, a lake sauna, a friend’s.

Can friends join?

Yes. Family means whoever you sauna with, up to 12 people.

What does it cost?

It’s free for now.

Does it read my Health data?

No. If you turn it on, Saunafam saves your sessions to Apple Health as mindful minutes. It never reads from Health.

I used Arro. How do I turn off its old Health access?

iOS keeps the read permission Arro had, and the app can’t remove it for you. Open the Settings app, then Health, then Data Access & Devices, choose Saunafam and turn everything off.
