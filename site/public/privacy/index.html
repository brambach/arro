Saunafam privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Saunafam is a weekly sauna streak for your family or your sauna crew. Everyone goes when they can, and you keep one streak together. This policy says what Saunafam collects, why, who sees it and how to delete it.

Who we are

Saunafam is made by Bryce Rambach, an individual developer (“we”, “us”). Questions: support@saunafam.com.

The short version

  • Saunafam collects what it needs to run your family’s streak and nothing else.
  • Your sessions, cheers and comments are shown to your family and nobody else.
  • Apple Health is optional and write only. Saunafam never reads from Health.
  • No location, no contacts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from Settings in the app.

What we collect

Sign in with Apple. Your name, the email you chose to share (your real email or Apple’s private relay address) and your Apple user id.

Your family. The family name, your member name, your photo if you add one, the color Saunafam gives you, your time zone and your reminder settings. Your time zone is used to work out your week. It isn’t your location.

Sessions. For each sauna session: the time, the minutes, the rounds, the sauna type, and, if you add them, the temperature, the humidity, a cold plunge and its minutes, a place you type (such as “Home” or “Gym sauna”), a note and a photo. When you log a session for people who sat with you, it’s saved for each of them too.

Cheers, comments, nudges and breaks. What you send, who you sent it to and when. For a break, the day it starts and the day it ends. Saunafam never asks why.

A push token, if you allow notifications. An address Apple gives Saunafam for sending notifications to your phone. Saunafam uses it to tell you when someone in your family has a sauna, logs one with you, cheers or comments on yours, nudges you or joins, and to send a weekend check-in. These aren’t sent between 9 p.m. and 7 a.m. in your time zone. Each notification’s text is kept for up to 7 days, then deleted. Reminders and the timer’s notifications are set up on your phone and never leave it.

What we don’t collect

  • No location. A place is only ever text you type. Saunafam never asks for location permission.
  • No reading from Apple Health.
  • No contacts.
  • No ads, no analytics tools, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Apple Health

If you turn it on, Saunafam saves each session to Apple Health as mindful minutes. It’s off until you choose it, and you can turn it off in Settings. Saunafam never reads from Health, and Health data is never used for ads or marketing, sold or shared.

If you used Saunafam when it was called Arro, iOS may still list the read permission Arro asked for, and the app can’t remove it. To turn it off, open the Settings app, then Health, then Data Access & Devices, choose Saunafam and turn everything off.

Saunafam Plus

If you buy Saunafam Plus, Apple handles the payment. RevenueCat, our purchase processor, tells us which Apple purchases you’ve made so your family gets Plus. We never see your card details.

Who sees your data

Your family. Only the people in your family see your name, color, photo, sessions, cheers, comments and breaks. Families are joined by invite code only. Photos are stored privately and shared with your family through links that expire.

The services that run Saunafam, each on our behalf and for nothing else:

  • Supabase stores Saunafam’s data, on servers in the United States.
  • Expo delivers push notifications. It receives your push token and the notification’s text, and passes them to Apple.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, Apple Health and notifications, under Apple’s own privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat processes Saunafam Plus purchases. It receives your Saunafam user id and your Apple purchase history for Saunafam.

Nobody else, unless the law requires it. We don’t sell your data.

If you used Arro

Saunafam used to be Arro. Your account, family, members, colors and profile photo carried over. Arro’s workouts, cheers, comments and workout photos are hidden in Saunafam and will be deleted in a coming update.

Deleting your account

Open Settings, Delete account in Saunafam. That removes your account, your place in your family, your sessions, photos, cheers, comments, nudges, breaks and push tokens, and the link to your Apple ID. If you were the last person in your family, the family is deleted too. Data can stay in our hosting provider’s backups for a short time before it’s overwritten.

The person who started your family can remove someone from it. That deletes the person’s sessions, photos, cheers, comments, nudges and breaks in that family. Their account stays, so they can join another family.

Children

Saunafam isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we’ll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, export or delete your data. You can see and change most of it in the app. For anything else, email support@saunafam.com and we’ll answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
