Motion principles
· Calm entrances — cards fade & rise on view, never bounce.
· Streak numerals count up once, then rest.
· Glow = meaning — only kept-today, milestones, progress.
· Cheers feel warm — tap gives a gentle pop + a rising 🧡.
· No confetti, no spinners — warmth over spectacle.
